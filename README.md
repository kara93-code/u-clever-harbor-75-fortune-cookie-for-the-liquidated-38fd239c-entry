One original fortune-cookie line for a crypto trader who has just been liquidated:

> Trader, the stars promise you a great recovery. They meant your sense of humour, not your balance.

The joke: the fortune opens with the classic oracular promise — the stars, a great recovery — and the punchline redefines "recovery" as the trader's sense of humour coming back, not the money. The loss is in the last word (balance), the trader is addressed up front, and nothing lectures or pities them.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) builds the line from an oracular opening and its turn, checks the contest gate — UTF-8 text, 1 to 160 characters, at most 20 words, one non-blank line with no leading or trailing white space — writes it to `/output/entry.txt` as UTF-8, forces mode 0644 so the grader can read it, and reads it back to confirm the disk copy matches the checked line.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
